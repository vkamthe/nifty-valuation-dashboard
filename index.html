Valuation till 2026 end

Where four Nifty indices sit today versus their own trend growth since the 2024 peak.

How to read this dashboard ▾

Each dial asks one question: has the index grown faster or slower than its own long-run rate since it last peaked?

Every one of these indices set an all-time high (ATH) in 2024 — about two years ago. If an index had simply kept compounding at its historical CAGR over those two years, it would now sit at its trend value:

trend value = 2024 ATH × (1 + CAGR)2

The needle is the current market level. Reading it against the trend value tells you whether the index is running ahead of, or behind, its own historical growth path.

The three marks on each dial

Floor (left end)
20% below the 2024 ATH — the scale starts here; anything cheaper is off-scale.
ATH
the 2024 all-time high itself.
Trend
the ATH grown two years at the index's CAGR — the "fairly grown" level.

The colour bands

Deep green
at or below the 2024 ATH — hasn't even reclaimed its old peak.
Green
above the ATH but below the trend value — recovered, but hasn't outrun two years of expected growth.
Red
above the trend value — has grown faster than its own historical rate; running hot.

Reading it honestly

The trend line is anchored to a cyclical peak, so it sets an aggressive bar — a needle in green is not automatically an "undervalued" signal, it mostly reflects that the market has spent two years growing into its 2024 valuations. CAGR is the only adjustable input: change it to see how sensitive each reading is to the growth assumption. This is illustrative modelling, not investment advice.
